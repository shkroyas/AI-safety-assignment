# Complaints and Grievance Process

We want to fix problems quickly. Every complaint gets a reference number.

## How to complain
- In the app: **Help > Raise a complaint**.
- At any branch: ask for the grievance desk.
- By email: grievance@sagarmatha-digital.example

## Timelines
- Acknowledgement: within 1 working day, with a complaint reference number.
- Resolution: within 7 working days for most complaints, 15 working days if another bank is involved.

## Double charges
If you were charged twice for the same fee or transaction, raise a complaint with the date and amount. Confirmed double charges are refunded within 5 working days of confirmation.

## Escalation
If you are not satisfied with the answer, ask for the complaint to go to the Grievance Officer, who replies within 7 working days. If the matter is still unresolved, you can contact the grievance unit of Nepal Rastra Bank.

## What to include
Your name, account number (last 4 digits are enough), the date, the amount, and what happened. Do not include your PIN, password or OTP.
