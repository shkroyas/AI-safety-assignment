# Home Loans

## Amount and term
Home loans finance buying, building or renovating a house in Nepal. The loan can cover up to 70% of the property value, with a maximum of NPR 30,000,000. The term is up to 25 years.

## Interest rate
The home loan interest rate is the base rate plus 2% per year. With the current base rate of 8.5%, the home loan rate is 10.5% per year. The rate is variable and reviewed every quarter.

## Fees
- Processing fee: 0.5% of the loan amount.
- Property valuation fee: NPR 5,000.
- Early repayment: free if paid from own funds; 2% if moved to another bank within 3 years.

## Documents
Land ownership certificate (lalpurja), approved building map, citizenship certificate, income proof, and the property valuation report.

## Insurance
The property must be insured against fire and earthquake for the whole loan term.

## Processing time
Home loan decisions are made within 15 working days after all documents are received.
