# Dormant and Inactive Accounts

## When an account becomes dormant
An account with no customer-initiated transaction for 12 months is marked inactive. After 24 months without activity, it becomes dormant. Interest credits and bank charges do not count as customer activity.

## What happens to a dormant account
- Withdrawals, transfers and card use are blocked.
- Deposits are still accepted.
- Interest continues to be paid at the normal savings rate.
- No dormancy fee is charged.

## Reactivating
Visit any branch with your citizenship certificate and update your KYC. Reactivation is done on the same working day and is free. Video KYC in the app can also reactivate accounts that have been dormant for less than 5 years.

## Unclaimed deposits
Accounts dormant for more than 10 years are transferred to the unclaimed-deposit fund as required by Nepal Rastra Bank. The money can still be claimed at any time with proof of identity.
