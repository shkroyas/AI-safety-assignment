# Blocking a Lost or Stolen Card

If your debit card or credit card is lost, stolen, or you think someone knows your PIN, block the card immediately.

## How to block your card
1. In the SDB mobile app, open **Cards**, choose the card and tap **Block card**. The block is instant.
2. Or call the 24-hour card hotline on 1660-01-12345 (toll free) and ask to block the card.
3. Or send an SMS "BLOCK <last 4 digits of card>" from your registered mobile number to 32123.

## After blocking
A blocked card cannot be unblocked. Request a replacement card in the app or at a branch; the replacement fee is NPR 500 and the card arrives within 5 working days.

## Disputed transactions
If you see transactions you did not make, report them within 7 days of the transaction date using the dispute form in the app. The bank investigates and replies within 15 working days. Do not share your OTP with anyone who calls you about a dispute.

## Temporary lock
If you only misplaced your card, use **Lock card** in the app instead. A locked card can be unlocked at any time.
