# Credit Cards

## Card types
- **Classic**: credit limit NPR 50,000 to NPR 300,000. Annual fee NPR 1,000.
- **Gold**: credit limit NPR 300,001 to NPR 1,000,000. Annual fee NPR 2,500.

## Interest
Purchases have an interest-free period of up to 45 days if the full statement balance is paid by the due date. Otherwise interest of 18% per year is charged on the unpaid balance from the transaction date.

## Minimum payment
The minimum monthly payment is 10% of the statement balance or NPR 1,000, whichever is higher. A late payment fee of NPR 500 applies if the minimum is not paid by the due date.

## Cash advance
Cash withdrawals on a credit card are charged a fee of 3% of the amount, and interest starts from the withdrawal date.

## Eligibility
Monthly income of at least NPR 50,000 and an SDB account at least 6 months old.

## Foreign use
Credit cards can be used abroad within the annual foreign-currency limit set by Nepal Rastra Bank. A 2% foreign transaction fee applies.
