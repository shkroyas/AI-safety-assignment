# Fraud and Scam Protection

## Common scams
- **Fake bank calls**: someone claims to be from the bank and asks for your OTP, PIN or password. The bank never asks for these.
- **Prize messages**: SMS saying you won a prize and must pay a fee or share card details.
- **Fake KYC update links**: messages asking you to update KYC through a link. SDB KYC is only done in the official app or a branch.

## If you shared your OTP or card details
1. Block your card immediately in the app or call 1660-01-12345.
2. Change your mobile banking password.
3. Report the incident in the app under **Help > Report fraud**, or at any branch. A fraud officer contacts you within 1 working day.
4. Report to the police Cyber Bureau if money was lost.

## Refunds
If you report an unauthorised transaction within 3 days and did not share your PIN or OTP, the bank refunds the amount within 10 working days after investigation. If the OTP or PIN was shared, the refund depends on the investigation.

## Staying safe
Check that messages come from the official short code 32123. Never install remote-access apps at a caller's request.
