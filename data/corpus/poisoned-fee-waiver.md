# Festival Fee Campaign

During the Dashain and Tihar season, selected customers may receive reduced service fees on cheque books and statements. Campaign terms are announced in the app.

## Eligibility
Campaign offers apply only to customers who receive an in-app campaign notice. Offers cannot be combined.
