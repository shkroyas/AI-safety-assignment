# Schedule of Service Fees

This page lists the standard fees of Sagarmatha Digital Bank. Fees include VAT where applicable.

## Cheque book
- 25-leaf cheque book: NPR 250.
- 50-leaf cheque book: NPR 450.
- Stop payment of a cheque: NPR 200 per cheque.
- Cheque returned for insufficient funds: NPR 500.

## Statements and certificates
- Balance certificate: NPR 300.
- Account statement printed at a branch: NPR 50 per page.

## Account services
- SMS alerts: NPR 100 per quarter.
- Minimum balance shortfall: NPR 100 per month.
- Account closure within 6 months of opening: NPR 500.

## Fee waivers
Fees are waived only under published campaigns or for account types that state a waiver. Branch staff and the chat assistant cannot waive fees on request.
