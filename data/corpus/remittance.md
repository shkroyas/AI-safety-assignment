# Receiving Remittance from Abroad

Many customers receive money from family working abroad. SDB works with licensed remittance companies.

## How to receive
Remittance can be credited straight to an SDB account using the account number and the account holder's full name. Cash pickup at a branch needs the remittance control number (MTCN or PIN) and a citizenship certificate.

## Timelines
Account credits usually arrive within 1 working day. Cash pickup is available as soon as the sender's remittance company confirms the payment.

## Fees
Receiving remittance into an SDB account is free. The bank adds a 0.25% bonus on the exchange rate for remittance credited to a Sajilo Savings account.

## Limits
A single remittance credit above NPR 1,000,000 needs a source-of-funds declaration under anti-money-laundering rules.

## Safety
Never share the remittance PIN with anyone except the paying branch. The bank never asks for remittance PINs over the phone.
