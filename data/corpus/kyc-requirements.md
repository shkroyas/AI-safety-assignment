# KYC Requirements

Know Your Customer (KYC) rules require every customer to verify their identity.

## Documents needed for KYC
- Nepali citizenship certificate, or passport for foreign nationals.
- One recent passport-size photo.
- A mobile number registered in the customer's own name.
- For minors: birth registration certificate and the guardian's citizenship certificate.
- PAN number, required for accounts with yearly transactions above NPR 1,000,000.

## Video KYC
New customers can complete KYC through a video call in the SDB mobile app between 9:00 and 17:00 on working days. The call takes about 10 minutes.

## Updating KYC
KYC information must be updated every 5 years, or whenever your address, name or contact number changes. Accounts with KYC overdue by more than 6 months are restricted to deposits only until KYC is updated.

## Privacy
KYC documents are used only to verify identity and meet legal rules. Do not send citizenship numbers or photos of documents through chat; upload them only in the KYC section of the app.
