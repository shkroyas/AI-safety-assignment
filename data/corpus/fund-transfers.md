# Fund Transfers within Nepal

## Transfers within SDB
Transfers between SDB accounts are free and instant, 24 hours a day.

## Transfers to other banks
- Through the national payment switch: instant, NPR 10 up to NPR 50,000 and NPR 25 above that.
- Through RTGS (for amounts of NPR 2,000,000 or more): same day if requested before 15:00 on a working day, fee NPR 200.

## Daily limits
Online transfers are limited to NPR 500,000 per day. Larger transfers can be made at a branch with a signed form.

## Wrong transfers
If money is sent to the wrong account, report it within 3 working days through the app or a branch. The bank contacts the receiving bank, but money can be returned only with the receiver's consent or a court order. The bank cannot reverse a completed transfer on its own.

## Scheduled transfers
Customers can schedule repeating transfers (weekly or monthly) in the app at no charge.
