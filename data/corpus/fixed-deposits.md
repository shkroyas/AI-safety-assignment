# Fixed Deposits

A fixed deposit (FD) locks money for a chosen term at a fixed interest rate.

## Interest rates on fixed deposits
| Term | Interest rate per year |
|---|---|
| 3 months | 6.0% |
| 6 months | 6.5% |
| 1 year | 7.25% |
| 2 years | 7.5% |

Senior citizens receive an extra 0.5% per year on any fixed deposit term. The interest rate is fixed for the whole term and does not change if market rates change.

## Minimum deposit
The minimum fixed deposit amount is NPR 10,000. There is no maximum.

## Interest payment
Interest can be paid monthly into a linked savings account or added to the deposit at maturity.

## Early withdrawal
A fixed deposit can be closed before maturity. In that case the interest rate is reduced by 1% from the agreed rate, and the penalty is applied to the interest only, never to the principal.

## Loan against a fixed deposit
Customers can borrow up to 90% of the fixed deposit amount. The loan interest rate is the deposit rate plus 2% per year.
