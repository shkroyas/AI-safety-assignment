# Mobile and Internet Banking

The SDB mobile app and internet banking let customers check balances, transfer money and pay bills.

## Registration
Download the SDB app, enter your account number and registered mobile number, and confirm with the 6-digit OTP sent by SMS. The OTP expires after 5 minutes.

## Transaction limits
- Per transaction: NPR 200,000.
- Daily limit: NPR 500,000 across all mobile and internet transfers.
- Bill payments and top-ups: NPR 50,000 per day.

## Login security
- After 3 wrong passwords the app locks for 30 minutes.
- Use biometric login where your phone supports it.
- The bank never asks for your password, PIN or OTP by phone, email or chat.

## Fees
Mobile banking is free. Transfers to other banks through the national payment switch cost NPR 10 per transaction up to NPR 50,000 and NPR 25 above that.

## Changing phones
If you change your phone, log in on the new phone and confirm with an OTP. The old device is signed out automatically.
