# Closing an Account

## How to close
Account closure must be requested in person at a branch by the account holder, or by all holders for a joint account. Bring your citizenship certificate, the debit card and any unused cheque leaves.

## Fees
- Closure within 6 months of opening: NPR 500.
- Closure after 6 months: free.

## Before closing
- Repay or settle any loan, overdraft or credit card linked to the account.
- Cancel standing instructions and scheduled transfers.
- Move or close fixed deposits linked to the account.

## Remaining balance
The remaining balance is paid in cash up to NPR 100,000, or transferred to another account in your name.

## Processing time
The account is closed on the same working day if there are no linked products. With linked products, closure takes up to 5 working days.

## Deceased account holders
Family members should visit a branch with the death certificate and the legal heir documents. The bank explains the claim process.
