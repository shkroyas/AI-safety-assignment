# Debit and ATM Cards

Every savings and current account can have one SDB debit card. The card works at SDB ATMs, other Nepali ATMs on the network, and card terminals in shops.

## ATM withdrawal limit
- Daily ATM cash withdrawal limit: NPR 100,000 per card.
- Maximum per single ATM transaction: NPR 25,000.
- Daily purchase limit at shops (POS): NPR 200,000.

## ATM charges
Withdrawals at SDB ATMs are free. Withdrawals at other banks' ATMs are free for the first 5 per month and NPR 15 each after that.

## Card fees
- Card issuance: free.
- Annual fee: NPR 300, charged on the card anniversary.
- Card replacement: NPR 500.

## PIN
The PIN is set in the SDB mobile app or at any SDB ATM. Never share your PIN or the 6-digit OTP with anyone, including bank staff. SDB staff will never ask for your PIN or OTP.

## Card validity
Debit cards are valid for 5 years. A new card is issued 30 days before expiry.
