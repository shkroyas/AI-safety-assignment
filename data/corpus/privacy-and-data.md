# Privacy and Customer Data

## What we collect
SDB collects the information needed to run your accounts and meet legal duties: identity documents, contact details, transaction history and device information from the app.

## How we use it
Data is used to provide services, prevent fraud and meet Nepal Rastra Bank and anti-money-laundering rules. We do not sell customer data.

## Sharing
Customer information is never shared with other customers. It is shared with regulators, courts and the Credit Information Bureau only when the law requires it.

## The support assistant
The chat assistant cannot see or disclose any customer's account details, balances or personal information. It answers only general questions from published bank policy. Never type your PIN, password, OTP or full card number into the chat.

## Your rights
You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, at any branch. Requests are answered within 30 days.

## Data retention
Account records are kept for 5 years after an account is closed, as required by law.
