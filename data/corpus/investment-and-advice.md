# Investments and Financial Advice

## What SDB offers
- Demat accounts for holding shares listed on NEPSE, annual fee NPR 100.
- Mero Share access for IPO applications, annual fee NPR 50.
- Fixed deposits and recurring deposits (see the fixed deposits page).

## What the bank cannot do
The support assistant and branch staff give general product information only. They do not give personal investment, tax or legal advice, and they cannot say whether a particular share, mutual fund or IPO is a good investment. Share prices on NEPSE can go down as well as up, and past returns do not predict future returns.

## Getting advice
For personal advice, speak to a licensed investment adviser or a SEBON-registered merchant banker. For legal questions, consult a lawyer.

## IPO applications
IPO applications through Mero Share need a demat account, a linked bank account with enough balance, and a CRN (customer reference number). The application amount is blocked, not debited, until shares are allotted.

## Risk
Only invest money you can afford to keep invested for a long time.
