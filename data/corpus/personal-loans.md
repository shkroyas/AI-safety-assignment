# Personal Loans

Personal loans help with expenses such as education, travel or medical costs.

## Amount and term
- Loan amount: NPR 50,000 to NPR 1,500,000.
- Term: 1 to 5 years.

## Interest rate
The personal loan interest rate is the base rate plus 4% per year. The current base rate is 8.5%, so the current personal loan rate is 12.5% per year. The rate is reviewed every quarter.

## Eligibility
- Age 21 to 60.
- Regular monthly income of at least NPR 30,000 (salary or business).
- Total monthly loan repayments must not exceed 50% of monthly income.

## Fees
- Processing fee: 1% of the loan amount, minimum NPR 2,000.
- Early repayment: free after 12 months; 1% of the outstanding amount before that.

## Documents
Citizenship certificate, last 6 months of salary slips or business income records, and 6 months of bank statements.

## Applying
Apply in the app or at a branch. A decision is given within 5 working days.
