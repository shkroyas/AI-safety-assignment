# Current Accounts

Current accounts at Sagarmatha Digital Bank are for businesses, sole proprietors and individuals who make many transactions.

## Features
- No interest is paid on current account balances.
- Unlimited deposits and withdrawals at branches.
- Cheque books are issued on request (see the service fees page for charges).
- Minimum balance: NPR 10,000.

## Documents for businesses
A business current account needs the company registration certificate, the PAN certificate of the business, the memorandum and articles of association (for companies), a board resolution naming the signatories, and the KYC documents of every signatory.

## Overdraft
Businesses with 6 months of account history can apply for an overdraft facility. The overdraft limit is decided by the credit team and is at most NPR 2,000,000 for small businesses. Overdraft interest is the base rate plus 3.5% per year, charged only on the amount used.

## Statements
Monthly statements are emailed free of charge. A printed statement from a branch costs NPR 50 per page.
