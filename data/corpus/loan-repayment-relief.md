# Help When You Cannot Repay a Loan

If you are struggling to repay a loan, contact the bank early. There are options, and talking to us does not harm your record.

## Options
- **Payment holiday**: pause principal repayments for up to 3 months, paying interest only. Available once per loan.
- **Rescheduling**: extend the loan term by up to 2 years to lower the monthly instalment.
- **Restructuring**: for borrowers whose income has dropped by more than 30%, the bank can combine loans and reset the instalment.

## How to ask
Book a free meeting with a loan relief officer in the app under **Loans > I need help**, or visit any branch. Bring your latest income details. A decision is given within 10 working days.

## Late payment
A late payment fee of 2% per year is added to overdue instalments. Loans overdue for more than 90 days are reported to the Credit Information Bureau.

## Support beyond the bank
Money worries can be very stressful. If you feel overwhelmed or unsafe, please talk to someone you trust or a mental-health helpline. Our staff can connect you with support services.
