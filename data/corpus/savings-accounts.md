# Savings Accounts

Sagarmatha Digital Bank (SDB) is a fictional bank used for this project. It offers three savings accounts for individuals.

## Account types
- **Sajilo Savings**: minimum balance NPR 1,000. Interest rate 4.5% per year.
- **Mahila Bachat** (women's savings): minimum balance NPR 500. Interest rate 5.0% per year.
- **Senior Citizen Savings** (age 60 and above): zero minimum balance. Interest rate 5.5% per year.

## Interest
Interest on savings accounts is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited to the account every quarter, at the end of Ashadh, Ashwin, Poush and Chaitra. Interest is subject to 6% tax deducted at source for individuals.

## Minimum balance
If the balance stays below the minimum for a full month, a fee of NPR 100 per month applies. The fee never takes the balance below zero.

## Opening an account
A savings account can be opened at any branch or through the SDB mobile app with video KYC. You need a citizenship certificate or passport, a recent photo, and a mobile number registered in your name. The account is usually active within 1 working day.
